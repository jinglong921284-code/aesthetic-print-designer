## Commercial use

Commercial use is not granted by the public noncommercial license. To request any of the following, open a [Commercial License / Rights Buyout issue](https://github.com/jinglong921284-code/aesthetic-print-designer/issues/new?template=commercial-license.yml):

- a written commercial-use license;
- an exclusive commercial license;
- a copyright assignment or full rights buyout;
- custom commercial terms for a product, client project, enterprise workflow, paid service, course, or redistribution.

Do not post confidential commercial information in a public issue. Provide only a short project summary and ask to continue through a private channel.

No commercial, exclusive, or assigned rights are granted until a separate written agreement is signed by the relevant rights holder. Any exclusive license, assignment, or buyout remains subject to rights already granted under earlier releases.
