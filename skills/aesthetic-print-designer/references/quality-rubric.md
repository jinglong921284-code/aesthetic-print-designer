# Print Quality Rubric

Use this rubric when selecting a direction, approving a revision, or deciding whether artwork may enter physical sampling. Copy `assets/quality-evaluation-template.md` for the review record.

The weights and 65/80 thresholds below are configurable workflow defaults, not an industry certification, legal clearance, or substitute for a user-, customer-, company-, or regulator-approved standard. Use the approved project standard when one exists and record any changed thresholds.

## Scoring method

Score every criterion from 0 to 5 and calculate:

`weighted points = criterion weight × score / 5`

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Exceptional and fully resolved; evidence is clear |
| 4 | Pass; minor polish only |
| 3 | Revise; direction is viable but named issues remain |
| 2 | Weak; substantial rework is required |
| 1 | Major mismatch or unresolved risk |
| 0 | Absent, invalid, or directly contradictory |

Do not use the total score to override a hard gate.

## Weighted criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Review evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Brief fit | 12 | Theme, audience, product, material, season, output type, and exclusions |
| Originality and rights screen | 15 | No copied layout, protected identifier, character, brand signature, or near-duplicate expression |
| Motif system | 10 | Hero, secondary, connector, texture, and negative-space roles |
| Composition | 12 | Scale hierarchy, density rhythm, movement, balance, and breathing room |
| Colour and material fit | 10 | Palette hierarchy, ground relationship, intended fabric, use, and colour behavior |
| Mark quality | 8 | Edge, grain, opacity, texture, clarity, and intentional print character |
| Series value | 8 | Shared premise with meaningful differences rather than mechanical variants |
| Textile scale and direction | 10 | Physical scale, directionality, seams, garment zone, cutting, and fabric behavior |
| Repeat or placement integrity | 10 | Required repeat evidence or placement/engineering constraints for the selected architecture |
| Output truthfulness and traceability | 5 | Correct label, version, evidence, assumptions, and production status |

Total weight: 100.

## Hard gates

### Immediate fail

Reject regardless of score when any of the following is present:

- near-copy, protected identifier, logo, character, or recognizable reference layout;
- violation of an explicit brief exclusion or wrong production architecture;
- unwanted text, model, garment, frame, shadow, or perspective inside a repeat tile;
- artwork represented as validated or production-ready without the required evidence;
- corrupted, unreadable, missing, or wrongly versioned final file.

### Mandatory revise before sampling

Do not advance to physical sampling while any applicable item remains:

- visible seam, clipped motif, pinhole, overlap, width jump, grid, track, mirror diamond, pinwheel, face, cross symmetry, or repeated focal point;
- repeat candidate without edge-lock data, offset review, 3 × 3 review, and recorded visual status;
- placement, border, or engineered artwork without scale, orientation, anchor, named garment zone, or seam/bleed constraints;
- unapproved palette drift, wrong colour count, muddy mark, or motif hierarchy that collapses at intended physical scale;
- physical size, pixel size, DPI, colour mode, filename, or version mismatch;
- unresolved fabric/process risk that changes the design decision.

## Decision levels

### `Fail`

Assign when:

- an immediate-fail gate is triggered; or
- the weighted score is below 65; or
- the direction would require a new premise rather than a bounded revision.

Mark the direction `Fail` with a short reason. Archive, move, or delete files only when that action is separately authorized. Do not use the failed direction as a benchmark.

### `Revise`

Assign when:

- the weighted score is 65–79; or
- any applicable criterion scores 0–2; or
- a mandatory-revise gate remains open.

Name no more than three priority changes, their owner, and the next review state. Keep approved anchors fixed.

### `Pass to sample`

Assign only when all conditions are true:

- weighted score is at least 80;
- no immediate-fail or mandatory-revise gate is open;
- originality and rights screen scores at least 4;
- brief fit scores at least 4;
- every applicable technical requirement has evidence;
- open production variables are truthfully marked pending and do not change the sampling decision.

`Pass to sample` authorizes a physical strike-off or sample only. It does not mean bulk-production approval.

## Series selection

For a batch, score each direction independently, then compare:

- premise contribution;
- compositional distinction;
- colourway or product role;
- duplication with stronger directions;
- combined assortment balance.

Do not retain a lower-quality direction merely to reach a requested quantity. Mark feedback as `candidate signal`, `approved direction`, or `production benchmark`.

The originality and rights screen identifies creative risk only. It does not establish ownership, licensing, trademark clearance, or legal freedom to operate.
