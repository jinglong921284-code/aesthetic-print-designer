# Print Specification Sheet / 印花规格单

> **Stage / 使用阶段：** Design, merchandising, and pattern-room alignment.
>
> **Boundary / 边界：** This is a design-stage specification. It is not a supplier transfer, physical sample approval, or bulk-production approval. Move to the separate Production Handoff only when supplier, sample, transfer, or bulk-production work is explicitly requested and authorized.
>
> Fill every field with a confirmed value or `待确认`. A named-colour result remains a digital candidate until its required physical reference and actual-fabric review are complete.

## 6. Design-stage status / 设计阶段状态

`Ready for Sampling / 可进入打样` means design, merchandising, and pattern-room confirmations are recorded for the current artwork/specification version and no unresolved issue blocks sampling. Physical colour and actual-fabric review may remain pending for the sampling stage. Record outstanding sampling checks and their owners in Section 5. This status does not mean samples or bulk production have been approved, and does not itself start sampling or file transfer.
